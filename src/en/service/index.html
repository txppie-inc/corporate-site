VOICEATLAS / OVERVIEW

From patient voices to scientific evidence

Through a “Trust Loop” built with patient organizations, VoiceAtlas collects clinical data and biospecimens, then links genomic data with clinical records. It creates a connected path from research to diagnosis, treatment, and drug discovery.

Patient-driven Platform: Transforming Patient Voices into Scientific Evidence

HOW IT WORKS

Connecting research with drug discovery

We provide research institutions with access to a proprietary database built from clinical and genomic data collected and analyzed in partnership with patient organizations. We then connect promising findings and drug candidates with pharmaceutical companies to support clinical trials and new drug development.
